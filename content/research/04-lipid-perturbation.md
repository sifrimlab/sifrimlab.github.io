Lipids are extraordinarily diverse and central to cellular function, yet we still have a limited understanding of the genetic mechanisms that shape the cellular lipidome.
A major challenge has been the lack of scalable approaches that can connect defined genetic perturbations to changes in lipid composition.

**LipoGrid** arrays CRISPR/Cas9-perturbed cells on a micropatterned grid and sequentially captures their lipidome, by spatial mass spectrometry, and their gRNA identity, complemented by single-cell RNA sequencing of matched cell populations.
In a first screen we quantified 158 lipid species across 143 target genes.
Most knockouts measurably altered lipid composition, often in specific lipid classes.
The screen recapitulates known enzyme–substrate specificities and disease-associated loss-of-function phenotypes, and the joint transcriptomic and lipidomic profiles reveal compensatory feedback mechanisms that buffer the lipidome against genetic perturbation.

LipoGrid is a collaboration between five KU Leuven teams within LISCO: Thierry Voet, Jeroen Lammertyn, Johannes Swinnen, Bernard Thienpont and Alejandro Sifrim.
