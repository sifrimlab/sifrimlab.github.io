As members of the [Leuven Single-Cell Institute (LISCO)](https://lisco.kuleuven.be), we work closely with experimental groups to help them get the most from their single-cell and spatial data.
A key focus is end-to-end analysis pipelines for marker gene identification, from raw count matrices to biologically validated cell-type signatures.
These collaborations drive new methodological questions and ensure our tools are tested on real and diverse datasets.

- **Oligodendroglia in ALS.** A single-nucleus atlas of more than one million cells from human motor cortex and spinal cord shows that "disease-associated oligodendrocytes" are an activation state rather than a distinct subpopulation, and identifies *LRP2* as an oligodendrocyte marker that is elevated in ALS cerebrospinal fluid.
- **Neuron loss in Alzheimer's disease.** With the Lab of Neuropathology, a deep learning model trained on more than 100,000 annotated objects detects and classifies neurons in histological images with 95% accuracy.
- **Skin expansion.** Under mechanical stretch, fibroblasts re-enter the cell cycle, reduce collagen production and adopt an embryonic-like state that enables epidermal stem cells to self-renew.
- **Nutrient sensing in the gut.** Single-cell analysis helped decode how different nutrients activate distinct ensembles of enteric neurons.
